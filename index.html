Process

From first call to handover in four steps

Every engagement has a written scope, a fixed price and a named person you can message directly.

  1. Days 1–3

    Scope

    A 30-minute call, then a written scope and quote. For security work, signed authorization comes before any testing starts.

  2. Week 1 onward

    Build or test

    Two-week sprints for software and data, a fixed test window for security. You get a demo or findings digest every week.

  3. At delivery

    Hand over

    A tagged release, a live dashboard or a full report with a walkthrough. Source, queries and credentials go to you.

  4. 30 days after

    Retest and support

    We retest every fixed vulnerability and handle bugs found in the first month at no extra cost.

FAQ

Questions we hear on the first call

How big is a typical project?

Software MVPs usually run six to twelve weeks. Analytics projects run three to eight weeks. A web application penetration test takes one to two weeks of testing plus a few days for the report.

Will a penetration test disrupt our production systems?

We agree on the rules of engagement first. Where possible we test a staging copy, and on production we avoid destructive actions and throttle automated scans. We tell you before anything risky.

Can you work with our existing team and codebase?

Yes. We join your repository, tools and standups, follow your conventions and write code your engineers can maintain after we leave.

What do we need to provide for a data project?

Read access to the source systems, one person who understands the business question, and a few hours a week for review. We handle the rest, including documentation.

Do you offer ongoing support after delivery?

Every project includes 30 days of support. After that, you can choose a monthly retainer for maintenance, monitoring or recurring security tests, or simply call us when you need us.
